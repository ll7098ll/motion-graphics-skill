# Adaptive Structural Archetypes

Never force every motion graphics video into an identical 4-act mold. The narrative structure must be designed around the video's **duration, target platform, and communicative goal**.

---

## 1. Archetype A: Kinetic Bumper & Micro-Loop (3s – 6s)

- **Platform**: YouTube intro/outro, app splash screens, website loader.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 1:1.
- **Pacing**: Rapid, high-energy, singular focus.
- **Beat Structure (120 BPM, 6–12 beats / 180–360 frames)**:
  - **Beats 0–2**: Instant kinetic hook. A geometric shape or initial letter emerges with high initial velocity.
  - **Beats 2–6**: Dynamic transformation. Shapes merge, rotate, or draw out the emblem's contours.
  - **Beats 6–8+**: Authoritative logo lockup with brand name and micro-sound hit. If looping, seamless return to frame 0.

---

## 2. Archetype B: Vertical Social Reel / Shorts (8s – 15s, 9:16)

- **Platform**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (1080×1920).
- **Rule of Thumb**: Mobile viewers decide within 0.8 seconds whether to swipe away.
- **Pacing**: Ultra-punchy, high visual density, center-weighted safe area.
- **Structure**:
  - **0.0s – 1.0s (The Thumb-Stopper)**: Massive visual statement or unexpected bold claim centered in the frame. No slow fade-ins.
  - **1.0s – 8.0s (The Kinetic Showcase)**: 3 fast-paced feature hits (each 2 seconds). High contrast, kinetic text, direct product demonstrations.
  - **8.0s – 12.0s (The Proof / Metric)**: Tangible proof point (e.g. "+10x faster", "$0 to $1M", 4.9★ rating).
  - **12.0s – 15.0s (The Clear CTA)**: Tap link in bio, download prompt, or bold brand mark.

---

## 3. Archetype C: Classic Narrative Brand Film (15s – 30s, 16:9)

- **Platform**: Product launch keynotes, website hero banners, investor presentations.
- **Pacing**: Symphonic, deliberate, emotional build.
- **Structure**:
  - **The Departure / Origin**: Atmospheric establishment of tone and signature metaphor.
  - **The Substance / Journey**: Demonstrating the craft, real UI components, routes, or technological engine.
  - **The Elevation / Scale**: Macro perspective shift, expansive lighting, speed acceleration.
  - **The Iconic Payoff**: Full corporate identity reveal, slogan, and memorable freeze-frame poster state.

---

## 4. Archetype D: Multi-Chapter Explainer (30s – 60s)

- **Platform**: Dedicated landing page explainers, customer onboarding, product demo tours.
- **Pacing**: Measured, articulate, educational.
- **Structure**:
  - **Chapter 1: The Status Quo / Pain Point (0–8s)**: Visualize the friction of existing solutions.
  - **Chapter 2: The Architectural Paradigm Shift (8–20s)**: Introduce the new model via high-level diagrams or 3D metaphors.
  - **Chapter 3: Interactive Deep-Dive Demos (20–42s)**: 2–3 specific workflow walkthroughs using authentic UI components.
  - **Chapter 4: Ecosystem & Integration (42–52s)**: Show how it connects with the user's tools and world.
  - **Chapter 5: Brand Anthem & Sign-off (52–60s)**: Final brand identity, pricing/tier tease, and primary URL.

---

## 5. Archetype E: Continuous Shape Morph (Any Duration)

- **Pacing**: Hypnotic, tactile, seamless.
- **Structure**:
  - Exactly **one hero container** stays on screen for the entire film.
  - Never cuts. The container's width, height, corner radius, background, and contents morph continuously from one state to the next (e.g. Button → Check disc → Music player → Slider → Card → Command palette → Button).
  - Perfect for infinite loops on website backgrounds or booth displays.
